        My research focuses on **optimization and AI-driven design of wireless networks**, spanning algorithm development, data-driven network intelligence, and experimental validation on real-world 5G/Open RAN platforms. I am particularly interested in making wireless networks more efficient, adaptive, and autonomous.

        My research spans three interconnected directions:

        ### AI-Driven Network Intelligence and Automation

        Developing learning-based and optimization-driven approaches for network configuration, performance prediction, autonomous control, and automated testing of wireless systems.

        ### Resource Allocation and Network Optimization

        Designing efficient algorithms for radio resource management, scheduling, network slicing, and joint communication and computing resource allocation in 5G/6G networks.

        ### Open RAN and Experimental Wireless Systems

        Building and evaluating programmable wireless systems, including Open RAN architectures, integrated access and backhaul, and experimental platforms for validating network optimization and automation techniques.
