      text: |-
        My research focuses on **AI-native and optimization-driven design of large-scale wireless systems**.
        I develop principled and data-driven methods for resource allocation, network orchestration, and
        performance modeling, with a particular emphasis on **Open RAN** architectures, and I validate them
        on experimental 5G/O-RAN platforms built on OpenAirInterface.

        ### AI-RAN and Programmable Scheduling
        Co-locating AI and RAN workloads on shared GPU infrastructure requires new ways to share compute and
        orchestrate workloads. I work on optimization methods for GPU resource sharing and on open,
        programmable MAC scheduling for AI-RAN 6G systems (*MAC-Gyver*), as well as on LLM-driven generation
        and testing of RAN schedulers from operator intents (*ALLSTaR*) and AI agents for autonomous RAN
        research and testing (*GENESIS*).

        ### Machine Learning for RAN Configuration and Performance Prediction
        I study data-driven approaches that predict network performance and automate RAN configuration,
        including context-specific causal learning for RAN configuration (*BLINC*), predicting retransmissions
        to improve MCS selection, and inferring application-layer characteristics from lower-layer RAN metrics.

        ### Integrated Access and Backhaul and UAV-Assisted Networks
        I investigate joint routing, resource allocation, and energy optimization for Integrated Access and
        Backhaul (IAB) networks in Open RAN, together with UAV-assisted wireless communication systems,
        combining algorithmic optimization with practical implementation.

        ### Automated RAN Software Testing
        Continuous evaluation of fast-moving open-source RAN software is essential for reliable deployments.
        *RANalyzer* automates continuous RAN software evaluation and regression analysis.

        ### Network Slicing and QoS-aware Scheduling in 5G (Ph.D.)
        During my Ph.D. at IIT Palakkad I developed components of end-to-end network slice provisioning for 5G:
        slice request scheduling that maximizes revenue for service aggregators and infrastructure providers,
        QoS-aware scheduling policies for 5G base stations, and rate-control mechanisms that jointly satisfy
        user QoS, RAN resource utilization, and core bandwidth efficiency.
